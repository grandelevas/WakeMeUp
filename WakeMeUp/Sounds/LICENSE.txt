Wakito bundled alarm tones
============================

The WAV files in this directory (classic.wav, chime.wav, rise.wav, pulse.wav,
bell.wav) were synthesized by scripts/generate-sounds.py. The CAF files
(dawn.caf, beep.caf, bells.caf, digital.caf, morning.caf, birds.caf,
marimba.caf, sleepy.caf) were synthesized by scripts/generate_sounds.py.
They are not recordings of anyone else's music or sound effects.

author1.caf through author6.caf are the author's own recordings, converted
by scripts/convert_author_sounds.py. They are not part of this dedication.
