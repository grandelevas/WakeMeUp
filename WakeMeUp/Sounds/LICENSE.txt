WakeMeUp bundled alarm tones
============================

The WAV files in this directory (classic.wav, chime.wav, rise.wav, pulse.wav,
bell.wav) were synthesized by scripts/generate-sounds.py. They are not
recordings of anyone else's music or sound effects.
