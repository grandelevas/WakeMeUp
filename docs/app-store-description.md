## English

Less than a coffee a year

Promotional text (165 characters):

Annual plan, less than a coffee a year. Mateusz gives half of subscription proceeds to causes he chooses. Achievements shows the goal and proof. No in-app donations.

Keywords (68 characters):

alarm,wake,clock,snooze,group,friend,icloud,sound,theme,charity,help

Description:

Wakito is an alarm app with an annual subscription that costs less than a coffee a year. Mateusz, who makes the app, gives half of the subscription proceeds to causes he chooses. The Achievements tab shows the current goal, what has been given, and the proof he posts. The app does not collect donations. The only payment is the App Store subscription, when it is turned on.

Alarms use AlarmKit, so a wake-up you set can ring full screen, including when the iPhone is locked. You can group alarms, turn a whole group off with one switch, and repeat every day, on weekdays, or on the weekend.

A friend can set an alarm for you after you accept an iCloud invite, and you can do the same for them. When the alarm rings, Play my song stops it and opens a Spotify, YouTube, or Apple Music link. You can use the included sounds or import and trim your own. Theme, accent color, and app icon are in Settings.
